## 2. What Dawn Patrol is

Dawn Patrol is a surf forecast for spots you add yourself. It reads public marine models, buoys, tides and weather data, then gives each spot an hourly score from 0 to 100 and suggests the best daylight windows. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Your spots, notes, settings and alert addresses are stored on your own device, in your browser, and you can export them.

## 4. Forecast accuracy and data

Forecasts, scores, size estimates, tide and timing figures, and crowd estimates are computer-generated and approximate. Models can be wrong, especially for small short-period surf, local wind and days far ahead. We do not promise that any data is accurate, complete or current.

- **Demo data:** if the app cannot reach live services, it shows made-up numbers marked "Demo data". Do not use them to plan a session.
- **Crowd estimate:** a rough guess from the day, time and conditions. It is not a headcount.
- **Beach advisories and water quality:** public records that may be days or months old. Check official sources for today.
- **Offline copies:** saved forecasts may be out of date. The app shows how old they are.
- **Your feedback taps:** "Was the forecast right?" adjusts scores on your device only.
